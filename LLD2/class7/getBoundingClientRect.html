getBoundingClientRect() Visualizer

Scroll the page and watch how each card's position changes relative to the viewport. A card becomes visible when its top edge moves above the bottom edge of the viewport.

The condition we are visualizing

getBoundingClientRect().top gives the distance between the element's top edge and the viewport's top edge.

if (rect.top < window.innerHeight) {
  card.classList.add('visible');
}

When rect.top becomes smaller than window.innerHeight, the card's top edge has crossed into the viewport.
